Trapezius

How it relates to your fitness goals:

  • Posture: Strengthening the trapezius helps improve shoulder stability and prevents slouching.
  • Neck Health: The upper portion of the trapezius supports the neck and head, alleviating tension and stiffness.
  • Injury Prevention: Weak trapezius muscles can lead to shoulder impingement and rotator cuff injuries.

Biceps

Bicep Anatomy

Long Head: This part of the bicep starts near the shoulder blade and contributes to the width of your biceps on a front view.

Short Head: This part starts lower on the shoulder and contributes to the peak of your biceps.
